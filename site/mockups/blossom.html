Drill-down

  • Region switch sits at the top of the rail, so every level re-scopes together.
  • Level 3 items: a group, a cultivar, a street or a day. Each opens in the drawer, e.g. #forecast/peak/yoshino.
  • Off-season (as now) the bloom tile says so and points to the first plums, rather than showing an empty map.

Data in this mockup

  • Quantile dates, group tree counts and the bloom ribbon come from forecast.json, trees.json and meta.json.
  • Streets from streets.json; cultivars and tiers from cultivars.json; accuracy from backtest.json.
