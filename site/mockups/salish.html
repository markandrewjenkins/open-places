Drill-down

  • Level 3 items: a hydrophone, a call, a sighting, a vessel class. Each opens in the drawer, e.g. #calls/tiers/bush-point-1103.
  • The "live" strip stays in the top bar, so status is visible from every level.
  • Ships archive lags (latest day Jun 19); the tile says so in its date stamp rather than calling it "today".

Data in this mockup

  • salish.json: hydrophones, reports, tiers, outlook, freshness.
  • ships/index.json: daily AIS vessel counts by category.
